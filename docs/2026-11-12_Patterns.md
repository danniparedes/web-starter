# Section Patterns

Every reusable section in the starter. Each is a `<section class="section section--NAME">` with a `<div class="column">` inside.
Copy the block, replace the `[placeholders]`, delete what you do not need.

## Page frame (on every page, identical)

| Pattern | Class | Structure | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | `section--header` | `.thirds`: logo, nav, social icons | `main.js` (mobile menu) |
| Footer | `section--footer` | `.thirds`: logo, sitemap, company info + small social icons; legal line below | `main.js` (year) |

## Layout inspiration

New layouts are inspired by patterns seen on Pinterest and similar boards; they are built fresh from our own tokens. Never copy a specific design, theme or template. Inspiration only.

## Layouts available inside a column

`.thirds` (3 parts), `.halves` (2 parts, `halves--top`), `.grid` (`grid--narrow`, `grid--projects`), `.bento`, `.stack` (`stack--loose`), `.column--narrow`.

## Dark theme

`section--dark` on one section, or `class="theme-dark"` on `<body>` for the whole site. Change the look only in the `--dark-*` tokens (`css/tokens.css`); never set colors inside a dark section. Every pair was checked for contrast on 2026-11-15; re-check after changing a value. The glass card is tinted dark on purpose, so white text stays readable over any photo.
