# Your website: tell us who you are

Thank you for trusting us with your website. This is not a test and there are no wrong answers. Short answers are fine, bullet points are fine, and "I'm not sure" is a good answer too.

**How long:** 30 to 40 minutes. You can write your answers, send voice notes, or we can go through them together on a call.

**Why we ask:** a website that looks like everyone else's does not help you. These questions help us build one that looks, sounds and feels like you.

**One request:** for any date, price, number, award or achievement you want on the site, please send a document or a link that shows it. We publish only what can be shown.
